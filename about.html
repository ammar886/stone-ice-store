Our approach

Northwind Goods started as a weekend market stall and grew into a small online shop focused on durable, understated pieces. We work with makers who care about materials and transparent sourcing — nothing flashy, just objects that feel good to live with.

This storefront is a demo: prices and inventory are placeholders. In a real deployment we’d connect checkout, shipping, and inventory behind the scenes.
